About Me

A brief introduction about who I am, what I’m learning, and where I’m heading.

My name is Abhishek G Nair, and I am a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) graduate with a strong interest in cybersecurity and information technology. I am currently focused on building a solid foundation in system security, Linux environments, networking, and ethical hacking concepts.

My journey into cybersecurity started with curiosity about how systems work, how vulnerabilities are discovered, and how organizations protect their data. To strengthen my understanding, I actively practice using Linux distributions such as Linux Mint and Kali Linux, explore networking tools, and work on hands-on labs and scripts.

I have also worked on practical projects such as website cloning to understand web technologies, shell scripting for automation, and OSINT analysis to collect and document publicly available information from websites in a structured manner. These projects have helped me develop a practical and analytical approach to learning.

My goal is to grow into a skilled cybersecurity professional by continuously learning, practicing, and gaining real-world exposure to security concepts. I am particularly interested in ethical hacking, system security, and defensive security practices.
